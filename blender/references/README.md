Neutral A-pose, arms about 30 degrees away from torso, relaxed open hands with clearly separated small fingers, feet separated slightly. Black matte knit crew-neck sweater with ribbed collar/cuffs/hem, charcoal relaxed slightly cropped trousers, off-white low-top lace-up sneakers. Cute proportions approximately 3.75 heads tall, oversized rounded head, narrow shoulders, small soft hands. Match reference hair silhouette, layering and warmth; avoid round sausage-like locks. Soft neutral studio lighting with gentle ambient occlusion, even illumination suitable for reconstruction, subtle knit texture and matte clay skin. A single character only, centered and large, entire hair and shoes visible with a small margin. Truly transparent background. No room, furniture, laptop, pedestal, shadows beyond the subject, other characters, multiple views, lettering or labels.
